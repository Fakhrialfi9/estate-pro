# CRM terminology

**Contact** — canonical person/organization relationship record.
**Lead** — qualified CRM prospect linked to one Contact and a lifecycle state.
**Inquiry** — intake record representing a typed request from a public or authenticated source.
**Activity** — reusable CRM work item (task, follow-up, call, meeting, reminder or internal note).
**Communication** — provider-neutral message record; provider adapters belong to infrastructure.
**Conversion** — explicit workflow that turns an inquiry or qualified lead into the next owned-domain record.
**Merge** — explicit, authorized, transactional operation that preserves the surviving lead and its history/relations.
